## Data issue found (for M5)

**98 representatives have ≤ 3 Arabic words** (221 ≤ 5), e.g. بِهَذَا "with this [chain]",
جَلَسْتُ "I sat", لَقِيتُ أَنَسًا. These are isnad/matn split leftovers or "same as the
previous" references that M0–M3 did not attach to their parent hadith. They are not
distinct hadith and should be merged or dropped before typesetting.
